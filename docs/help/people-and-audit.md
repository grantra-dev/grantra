# People and audit

For Grantra admins, helpdesk and auditors.

## People

**People** finds anyone by name or email; filter by status to see who is starting soon, active, suspended or has left. A person's page shows their details from HR, their manager, their access and their accounts in each application.

Admins and auditors also see the person's Grantra roles, their joiner, mover and leaver events, and their audit history. Admins can:

- give or remove a Grantra role (admin, helpdesk, auditor), optionally until a date. Nobody can change their own roles.
- make the person eligible for just-in-time access.

When someone says their access is wrong, start here: the access list says where each piece came from (their job, a request, an admin), and the history says when it changed.

## Audit log

**Audit log** has every change and decision, newest first: who did it, to what, and the full details on demand. Use the filter to narrow the events loaded on the page. Admins and auditors see it.
