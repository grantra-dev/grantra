# Approvals and reviews

You see these pages when someone reports to you, when you own an application, or when you are a Grantra admin or auditor.

## Approvals

**Approvals** lists the requests waiting on you, soonest deadline first. For each one you see who asks, for what, why and for how long.

- **Approve** sends it to the next step, or gives the access when you are the last one.
- **Reject** needs a comment, which the person sees.

A manager decides first, then the application's owner. A request nobody decides in 7 days expires. When a request conflicts with access the person already holds, an admin has to accept the risk on the request itself, with a reason and for how long.

## Access reviews

Every so often an admin launches a **campaign**: managers review what their people hold, or owners review who holds their application. Open **Access reviews** to see what you are asked to review.

- **Keep** what the person still needs for their job.
- **Revoke** what they no longer need. Grantra takes it away. Revoking many at once asks you to confirm.

When unsure, ask the person or their manager before the campaign ends. Admins launch and close campaigns, and admins and auditors follow each campaign's progress.
